# Code of Conduct

Adapted from Contributor Covenant v2.1, shortened for OTA-Bot-Mochi.

---

## Pledge

We pledge to make participation in OTA-Bot-Mochi a welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of age, background, identity, ability, or experience level. We commit to acting with respect, empathy, and professionalism.

---

## Standards

Positive behavior includes:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Respecting different opinions and experience levels
- Giving constructive feedback focused on the work
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing when mistakes happen

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Trolling, hate speech, or discriminatory remarks
- Publishing private information without consent
- Any conduct that disrupts collaboration

---

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions, comments, and issues that violate this Code. Repeated or serious violations may lead to temporary or permanent exclusion from the project.

---

## Contact

Report concerns via GitHub Issues on the OTA-Bot-Mochi repository. Do not include secrets or personal data in reports. All reports are reviewed confidentially and addressed within 7 days where possible.
